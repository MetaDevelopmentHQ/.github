## About

**Meta Development** is an independent development brand focused on building modern solutions for **FiveM** and **Discord**.

We are currently in the early development stage, building our infrastructure, internal standards, product structure and first public releases.

Our goal is not to publish as many products as possible.

We want to create systems that are:

- Well structured
- Practical to use
- Easy to configure
- Visually consistent
- Performance-conscious
- Maintainable over time

## Development Principles

We want every Meta Development project to follow a consistent development process.

```text
Idea
  ↓
Planning
  ↓
Development
  ↓
Testing
  ↓
Optimization
  ↓
Documentation
  ↓
Release
  ↓
Maintenance
```

### Our priorities

**Clarity**  
Projects should be understandable and easy to configure.

**Reliability**  
Features should behave consistently in real server environments.

**Performance**  
Unnecessary loops, events and database operations should be avoided.

**Security**  
Sensitive logic should remain server-side whenever possible.

**Maintainability**  
Projects should be structured for future updates rather than quick one-off releases.

**Presentation**  
Documentation, UI and product presentation are part of the product itself.
